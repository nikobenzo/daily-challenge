Delete your Daily Challenge account

Daily Challenge, by Mykola Yakovetskyi, lets you delete your account and all of its data at any time, either in the app or by email without the app.

In the app (Android or iPhone)

  1. Open Daily Challenge and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and read what will be deleted.
  4. Tap the Delete account… button below that list.
  5. Confirm with Delete account.

The account is deleted at once, and you are signed out on every device.

Without the app, by email

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email yakovetskyi@icloud.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Daily Challenge account”. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and the protected password hash.
  • Your challenge: the start date and time zone, every water amount, workout, walk, diet and reading mark, your extras, and the time and device identifier of each entry.

This cannot be undone, and nothing is kept on our servers. Settings such as reminder times and appearance were only ever stored on your devices; remove the app to remove them. Our hosting provider’s routine backups, if any, expire on their own schedule, and a deleted account is never restored from them.

Only want to stop on one device?

Choose Sign out in Account instead. That removes the session from that device and keeps your account and history.

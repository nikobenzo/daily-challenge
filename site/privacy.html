Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Daily Challenge (the “app”) is a small tracker for a 75-day daily challenge, on iPhone, Android and Mac. This page explains what the app collects, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. The short version: the app keeps your account email and your challenge entries so they can sync between your devices. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Who is responsible

Mykola Yakovetskyi runs Daily Challenge and is responsible for your data. Contact: yakovetskyi@icloud.com.

What the app collects

Water reminders are local notifications created on your device. They do not pass through any server, and the app only asks for permission to show them if you turn reminders on.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking: your data is never combined with data from other apps or websites, and never sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs that collect usage data. The only network service the app talks to is Supabase.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health records or camera or microphone access.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores your account and challenge entries on our behalf. Every row is protected so that only your own signed-in account can read or change it.
  • Resend delivers the account emails above.
  • Apple provides the App Store, TestFlight and your device’s backups. If you back up your device, Apple may store the app’s local copy under your own Apple account.
  • Google provides Google Play. The Android app turns off Android backup and device transfer for its data, so no copy of it goes to your Google account.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and challenge entries until you delete your account. Local copies stay on a device until you sign out and remove the app or delete your account.

Deleting your account and data

In the iPhone or Android app, open Account and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your email address and all of your challenge entries from our servers, and cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app, email yakovetskyi@icloud.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Signing out on a device removes the local session from that device only. Step-by-step instructions, with or without the app: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing yakovetskyi@icloud.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner (jerseyoic.org) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what the app collects we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.

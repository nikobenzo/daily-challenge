Daily Challenge support

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email yakovetskyi@icloud.com. Tell us which device you use (iPhone, Android phone or Mac) and the app version (Account, then Version), and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

I did not get my confirmation or reset code

Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then tap Resend code. Make sure the address is typed correctly. If it still does not arrive, email us.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password?, enter your email, then type the code we send together with a new password (at least 10 characters with a letter and a number). You can also change it later under Account.

What counts as a complete day?

Meeting all five of your daily goals: water, two activities, reading, and keeping to your diet. By default these are four litres of water, a 45-minute workout, a 45-minute walk, 10 Bible pages and a clean diet. Your optional “extras” never count towards a complete day or your streak.

Can I change my daily goals?

Yes. In setup, or later under Account and Daily goals, you can rename the two activities, the reading goal and the diet, give the activities and reading an amount, unit and icon, and set your own water goal (1 to 5 litres) and glass size. A change applies from today, and earlier days keep the goals they had. There are always five goals, and the 75 days do not change.

Can an extra repeat?

Yes. When you add or edit an extra, choose how often it appears: once, every day, on weekdays, on days you choose, or every few days. A repeating extra is never marked Late and never carries over; each day it appears is ticked on its own. A one-off extra still carries over, marked Late, until you finish it.

Why does the app say my data is saved on this device only?

The app saves everything on your device first and syncs when it can reach the server. Entries are never lost while offline and upload on the next sync.

The widgets do not update

Open the app once so it can refresh, and check that you are signed in. Widgets show today’s entries from the app; they never contact the server themselves.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone or Android app go to Account and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your entries. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account. See Delete your account and the privacy policy.

Install Daily Challenge on a Mac

The Mac app is not in the App Store. To install it, open Terminal and paste this line:

curl -fsSL https://nikobenzo.github.io/daily-challenge/install.sh | bash

It downloads the latest version, checks that it is signed by the developer and notarized by Apple, puts it in Applications and opens it. Nothing is installed if a check fails. Prefer to do it by hand? Download the zip from the latest release, unzip it, drag Daily Challenge to Applications and open it. It needs macOS 14 or newer on an Apple silicon Mac.

Daily Challenge support

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email yakovetskyi@icloud.com. Tell us which device you use (iPhone or Mac) and the app version (Account, then Version), and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

I did not get my confirmation or reset code

Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then tap Resend code. Make sure the address is typed correctly. If it still does not arrive, email us.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password?, enter your email, then type the code we send together with a new password (at least 10 characters with a letter and a number). You can also change it later under Account.

What counts as a complete day?

Four litres of water, a workout, a walk, Bible reading, and keeping to your diet. Your optional “extras” never count towards a complete day or your streak.

Why does the app say my data is saved on this device only?

The app saves everything on your device first and syncs when it can reach the server. Entries are never lost while offline and upload on the next sync.

The widgets do not update

Open the app once so it can refresh, and check that you are signed in. Widgets show today’s entries from the app; they never contact the server themselves.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone app go to Account and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your entries. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account. See the privacy policy.

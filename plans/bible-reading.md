# Bible Reading

## Agreed behavior

Read at least 10 pages of the Bible daily. One-tap self-certified completion; no page counter, reading journal, book/chapter tracking, or reading timer in this release.

Pending and complete states can be reversed. An unconfirmed closed day fails. Allow explicit historical corrections and recalculate streaks.

## Interface

A compact accessible reading icon beneath the jug. Tooltip/accessibility description states 'Read 10 Bible pages'; avoid a generic reading label that hides the agreed requirement.

## Acceptance

- Completion is scoped to the selected challenge date.
- Reading must be complete alongside all four other requirements for daily success.
- Reversal and past-day corrections affect derived streaks appropriately.
- Shared conflict rules apply to offline edits.
- No reading reminders.

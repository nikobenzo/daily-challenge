# Walk

## Agreed behavior

A 45-minute walk is required every day, independently of the workout requirement. One-tap self-certified completion; no timer, route, step counter, or location tracking.

Pending and complete states can be reversed. An unconfirmed closed day fails. Past-day corrections are allowed and recalculate the streak.

## Interface

A compact accessible walk icon beside the other non-water habits. Tooltip/accessibility description explains the 45-minute requirement; completion is distinguishable without color.

## Acceptance

- Toggling the walk changes only the selected day's walk requirement.
- A completed workout does not automatically complete a walk.
- A pending walk prevents full-day completion even when all other requirements are met.
- Offline edits and corrections follow shared conflict rules.
- No walk reminders in this release.
